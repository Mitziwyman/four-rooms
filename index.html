ACBT Breathing
when you're ready — no pressure
Phase 1 — Breathing Control

Relax. Gentle lower-chest breathing at your own pace. 5–10 breaths. This is rest.

Phase 2 — Thoracic Expansion

Three to five slow deep breaths. Breathe in fully, hold 2–3 seconds, breathe out gently without forcing.

Phase 3 — The Huff

One or two huffs — not coughs. Open mouth, squeeze air out as if steaming a mirror. Then return to Phase 1.

Repeat for 10–20 minutes, or until clear. Once a day to start.
